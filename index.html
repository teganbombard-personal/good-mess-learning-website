Learning is supposed to be a good mess.

Good Mess Learning is a homeschool resource and coaching hub built on the idea that real learning is hands-on, curious, and a little chaotic, in person in Central Florida and virtually anywhere. Plus coaching to help parents navigate it all.

Three ways to learn with us

Whatever your family needs, from a full program to a little extra support to just a sounding board, there's a way in.

In-Person Classes

Small-group classes in Clermont / Lake County built around hands-on, real-world learning: kids making, testing, building, and getting messy with the material, not just reading about it.

Virtual Classes

Live and async classes for K-12 students anywhere in the country. Same hands-on philosophy, delivered wherever you are.

Parent Coaching

1:1 coaching and planning sessions to help you build a homeschool plan that actually fits your family, not someone else's.

Built by someone who's lived it

A FL-certified teacher with 15 years in the classroom, and a homeschool mom of 10 years who has spent the last 8 building personalized learning experiences and coaching for families across the country (and beyond). Good Mess Learning is philosophy-first: we start with how kids actually learn, then build the program around that.
